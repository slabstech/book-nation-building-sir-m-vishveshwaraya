The old order is change. the Congress party is in control of the administration in seven Provinces of British India and,
in the seven out of the eleven at least, a Government of the people for the people seems established. 
How far it is also a government by the people has yet to be tested. 
The precise extent of political power transferred to the elected representatives of the people is still in doubt,
but, in what follows, it will be assummed that the unstinted co-operation of the British authorities will be
at the disposal of these new governments for all constitutional activties connected with the nation building,
in spite of the defective provisions of the Government of India Act of 1935 in this regard.
